\textbf{Question and design.} Does document-ID coverage suffice to explain
answer quality, or do evidence use and evaluation introduce separate failures?
We uniformly sample 20 of the 121 Qwen practice answers with three judge
dimensions all zero, without replacement from sorted IDs using Python
\texttt{random.Random(20261118)}. This is exploratory sampling, not a
preregistered intervention. We inspect saved questions, answers, judge reasons,
gold documents and the retrieved passages implicated by each diagnosis.
Dense5 paired records are retained for inspection, not a new significance test.
No dev answers or new model/API calls are used. Review is AI-assisted and
unblinded; the authors subsequently confirmed three representative case analyses.
This does not supply an exhaustive, mutually exclusive mechanism taxonomy
for all 20 cases or an estimate for all queries/errors.

\textbf{Descriptive labels and evidence status.}
We retrospectively recode the existing source-linked notes into five
non-exclusive flags (Table~\ref{tab:diagnostic-flags}); these include evaluation
and reference issues, not just generator errors. \emph{Confirmed} means one of
the three representative evidence-chain observations confirmed by the authors,
not a validated causal mechanism or a fully adjudicated answer.
\emph{Possible} is an AI-reviewed, source-supported working diagnosis without
author confirmation; \emph{ambiguous} retains material uncertainty about question,
reference interpretation or attribution. All 20 have explicit flags and a status,
but the remaining 17 have not been reported as fully human-reviewed.

\textbf{Zero-score integrity and calibration.}
All 700 saved answers are nonempty and finish with \texttt{stop}.
Scores match both cached results and valid saved raw judge responses;
the parser raises on malformed scores and exhausted retries raise an error,
not a default zero. Zero scores therefore cannot be explained here by empty
outputs or silent parse-to-zero handling.
For dense, the zero-ID sets for correctness, grounding and completeness are
identical (138 each); for Qwen they are likewise identical (121 each).
There are no answers with only some dimensions zero. This synchrony warrants
calibration review, not an assumption that all three dimensions fail equally.
The paired zero/nonzero counts are 85/53/36/176 for
zero--zero/zero--positive/positive--zero/positive--positive.
Thus 53 leave zero and 36 enter it; the net reduction of 17 is not a universal
per-query improvement or a human-verified accuracy gain.

The frozen judge source, verified modulo LF/CRLF line endings, shows the user
prompt's format example as
\texttt{\{"correctness":0,"grounding":0,"completeness":0,\ldots\}}.
It may invite value copying/anchoring as well as specify JSON structure.
This is an \emph{untested prompt-design risk}: the present data do not identify
its contribution, or separate it from strict reference-only scoring, genuine
answer defects and other judge behaviour. No alternate prompt or rejudging is
run, and original scores are not replaced.

\textbf{Prompt integrity.} Rebuilding all 700 saved practice prompts from their
selected IDs reproduces full top-five corpus text and saved SHA-256 hashes
exactly; selection metadata also reports no truncation. This excludes local
context-builder prefix loss for these fresh runs, not remote-server truncation.
It does not establish that the source text itself is correct or sufficient.

\textbf{Three targeted follow-up candidates.}
In the Ireland pension case (\texttt{q\_371c6ca10bf1bf5e}), both gold records
are included. Other selected records explicitly discuss UK allowances
(\texttt{r\_1ada1a124c5a8a12}), US retirement accounts
(\texttt{r\_422ea5e23cebc8b1}) and company-tax treatment
(\texttt{r\_dc4b5d2c6048003d}). The answer presents contribution limits that
``can be carried forward and offset against future profits'' as an Ireland
benefit, without preserving the scope of those statements.
More specifically, the company-tax source says ``there will be a tax loss that
can be carried forward and offset against future profits''; the answer instead
says ``There are maximum contribution limits, but these can be carried forward
and offset against future profits''. This changes the subject of the rule,
independently of whether one adjudicates Irish tax law.
This is a traceable scope-blending observation, not a demonstrated causal
effect of distractors.

In the stocks-on-sale case (\texttt{q\_1024d33a7dfa33ef}), the answer adds
``Written Confirmation'' from a selected share-disposal/tax-basis document as
a stock-selection factor. In the car-financing case
(\texttt{q\_ce474d8f8d548797}), the second selected document states that dealers
care about immediate, risk-free payment; the answer instead combines general
cash/loan advice and omits that point. The short question also permits broader
interpretations, so omission is not definitive proof of interference.
These three cases motivate possible interventions; none measures snippet benefit.

\textbf{Conditions can matter more than brevity.}
The Tangerine case (\texttt{q\_66bb08d4774227f7}) has a short gold document
ranked first, yet the answer omits the recipient actions that start the
2--3-business-day interval. In \texttt{q\_e86dfaec0aa23a50}, the answer moves
gold/silver's usefulness from \emph{after} the crisis to \emph{during} it.
Keyword-oriented extraction could remove precisely these important conditions.

\textbf{Evaluation is a separate boundary.}
The implemented judge is instructed to evaluate only against gold documents;
the generator sees retrieved top-five documents. For example, the expense-ratio
answer (\texttt{q\_0a12138b81eb7e2a}) cites quarterly reports/prospectuses
supported by selected text outside gold. Such reference-scope differences
do not by themselves prove factual hallucination or judge non-compliance.

Six sampled cases have specific judge-text conflict flags:
the eBay reason acknowledges grounding despite all-zero scores;
the credit-report reason misstates the answer's attribution to creditors;
the Tangerine reason attributes wording absent from the answer;
the personal-tax reason describes a largely individual-deductions answer as
business-tax discussion; the software reason makes contradictory Bloomberg
support claims; and the crisis-assets reason denies items actually present in
gold. These are review flags, not six corrected scores or a general judge-error
rate. Real answer defects can coexist with faulty judge explanations.
All original scores and paired intervals are retained.

\textbf{Second source check, not independent adjudication.}
Three representative cases were checked again by the AI assistant against
named, verbatim source spans. The pension example supports subject substitution.
In the software example (\texttt{q\_e16177bbeb991130}), gold names MetaStock,
Incredible Charts and VectorVest and the answer includes all three;
Bloomberg wording comes from retrieved, non-gold text, despite the judge
attributing it to gold. This flags explanation fidelity and calibration,
not a fully correct answer. The crisis-assets example establishes two concurrent
issues: the answer changes \emph{after} to \emph{during}, while the judge denies
helicopters/connections that gold contains. A judge mistake therefore does not
vindicate the entire answer. The three checks and exact quotations are saved
in \texttt{error-case-recheck.json}; the authors have confirmed these three
analyses. This confirmation does not establish the prevalence of mechanisms
or the causal effect of removing evidence.

\textbf{Reproduction and stopping boundary.}
\texttt{python3 rag/extensions/analyze\_practice\_errors.py} reconstructs the
sample, coverage counts and review files using the course asset archive and
saved practice outputs. The evidence record lists the script, editable
annotations, full case packets and source-hash manifest.
Every quoted review span is checked verbatim against its named source;
this checks provenance, not the correctness of the interpretation.
We stop configuration search rather than add a snippet arm without a specific
test. A future intervention must preserve conditions, control content/length
changes and identify any gold-aware extraction as an oracle diagnostic.
